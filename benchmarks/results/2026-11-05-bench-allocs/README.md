# 2026-11-05, reference machine: allocators compared

Measured before `benchmarks/run.sh` existed, with the one-off script [`allocs.sh`](allocs.sh), kept here as it was on the machine.

- **What:** `jc_qjmc` at two cutoffs, `kernels` cases B, C, D and F, and the main suite's `apply1` and `applyJC`, in five modes: glibc on huge pages (`base`), the second prototype (`proto2`), jemalloc 5.4.0 and tcmalloc (gperftools 2.19) preloaded into the binaries of `base`, and glibc on 4 KB pages. Five rounds alternating, pinned to core 1.
- **Source:** `base` is the library at 14254a1 (the build of `2026-11-04-bench-remeasure`) and C++QED 0a0efb12 with Eigen 3.4.0; `proto2` as in `2026-11-04-bench-protothp2`; `jemalloc` and `tcmalloc` are the binaries of `base` with the allocator preloaded.
- **Machine:** the reference machine of [`performance.md`](../../performance.md), "Setup". Its state at the start and at the end is in `log`. The script did not record the compiler version; the page gives GCC 16.2.1 for the machine at the time, and Eigen 5.0.1, which the library's benchmarks used.
- **Quoted in `performance.md`:** the items on allocators of "Distance on huge pages": `jc_qjmc` at a 16 MiB state in 3210 ms with glibc, 2672 with jemalloc, 3384 with tcmalloc, 2621 with the prototype and 2877 on 4 KB pages, and the counts of kernel configurations slower than on 4 KB pages.
- **`times`:** one line per run of `jc_qjmc`: mode, mode cutoff, round, wall time in milliseconds. The trajectories that the runs wrote are not kept here; they are C++QED's output, not this library's.
- **`alloc.*.log`:** the large allocations of one run of `jc_qjmc` per allocator. They were written again after the session by [`logs2.sh`](logs2.sh) with [`bigalloc2.c`](bigalloc2.c), which also catches C++'s `operator new`; the logs of `allocs.sh` itself had missed the allocations of jemalloc and tcmalloc.

The benchmarks' files were renamed to the layout of `run.sh`, so that `benchmarks/reduce.py` reads them; their contents are unchanged. The script's mode on 4 KB pages (`nothp`) ran the binaries of `base`, so its files are those of `base` on `4k`:

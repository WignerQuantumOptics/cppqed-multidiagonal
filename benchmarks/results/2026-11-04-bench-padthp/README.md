# 2026-11-04, reference machine: does padding large allocations remove the slow distance?

Measured before `benchmarks/run.sh` existed, with the one-off script [`padthp.sh`](padthp.sh), kept here as it was on the machine.

- **What:** `jc_qjmc` at two cutoffs, `kernels` cases B, C, D and F, and the main suite's `apply1` and `applyJC`, in three modes: on huge pages, on 4 KB pages, and on huge pages with every large allocation padded by 1 KiB (`padalloc.so` preloaded). Five rounds alternating, pinned to core 1.
- **Source:** the library at 14254a1 (the build of `2026-11-04-bench-remeasure`) and C++QED 0a0efb12 with Eigen 3.4.0. The builds `base` and `pad` are the same binaries; `pad` differs by the preloaded [`padalloc.c`](padalloc.c).
- **Machine:** the reference machine of [`performance.md`](../../performance.md), "Setup". Its state at the start and at the end is in `log`. The script did not record the compiler version; the page gives GCC 16.2.1 for the machine at the time, and Eigen 5.0.1, which the library's benchmarks used.
- **Quoted in `performance.md`:** not by its figures. It is the first of the experiments for [#26](https://github.com/WignerQuantumOptics/cppqed-multidiagonal/issues/26) that led to the comparison of allocators in "Distance on huge pages".
- **`times`:** one line per run of `jc_qjmc`: mode, mode cutoff, round, wall time in milliseconds. The trajectories that the runs wrote are not kept here; they are C++QED's output, not this library's.
- **`alloc.pad.log`:** the large allocations of one padded run of `jc_qjmc`.

The benchmarks' files were renamed to the layout of `run.sh`, so that `benchmarks/reduce.py` reads them; their contents are unchanged. The script's mode on 4 KB pages (`nothp`) ran the binaries of `base`, so its files are those of `base` on `4k`:
